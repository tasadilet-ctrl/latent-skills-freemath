# Long multiplication, written out

When asked to multiply two whole numbers, do not estimate and do not compute the product in one step.

1. Write the second number's digits from right to left, with their place values (units, tens, hundreds, ...).
2. For each digit d of the second number, compute the partial product (first number × d) exactly, by multiplying digit by digit from the right and writing each carry. Then append the right number of zeros for the place value.
3. Write all partial products one per line, aligned.
4. Add them two at a time, column by column from the right, writing each carry, and keep a running total.
5. Check the number of digits against the estimate from the leading digits.
6. Give the final product without commas or spaces.
