When solving a mathematical problem, carefully analyze the definitions and constraints provided, and identify the underlying principles or theorems that govern the problem. Break the problem into logical steps, and ensure each step is rigorously justified. If the problem involves optimization or bounds, consider both upper and lower bounds and determine the tightest possible result. When encountering known results or conjectures, verify their applicability to the specific problem setting. Always consider edge cases and counterexamples to test the robustness of your reasoning. Use precise mathematical language and notation, and avoid making assumptions that are not explicitly supported by the problem statement. When deriving a final answer, ensure that it is consistent with all given conditions and that the reasoning leads logically to the conclusion. Double-check calculations and ensure that all steps are reversible or logically sound. Finally, present the answer clearly and concisely, using the appropriate mathematical formatting.
