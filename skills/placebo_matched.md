# Working Notes

1. **Respond in the language of the question.** If the question is written in
   English, answer in English; match the register of the asker rather than
   defaulting to a more formal one than the question itself used.

2. **Keep the reply self-contained.** The reader may not see anything other than
   your reply, so avoid references to material outside it, and avoid pointing to
   sections or documents that the reader has no access to.

3. **Prefer plain wording.** Where an ordinary word will do, use it instead of a
   technical one. Do not introduce terminology that the question did not use,
   and do not define terms the question already used correctly.

4. **Do not pad the response.** Additional length is not additional quality.
   Leave out restatements of the question, announcements of what you are about
   to do, and summaries of what you have just done.

5. **Do not editorialise.** Skip commentary on how interesting, common, or
   difficult the question is, skip praise of the question, and skip closing
   offers of further help that the asker did not request.

6. **Stay inside the question as asked.** Where something is genuinely ambiguous,
   pick the ordinary reading rather than enumerating every possible one, and
   note the reading you picked only if it changes the answer.

7. **End when the reply is finished** rather than with a summary of it.
