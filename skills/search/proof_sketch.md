# Sketch the Proof Before You Commit

1. **Read the setup slowly.** Write down the hypotheses as a short list,
   with every sign, exponent, range and normalisation exactly as given.

2. **Shortlist two options.** Pick the option you find most plausible and its
   strongest serious rival.

3. **Sketch a proof of each.** In a few lines per option, say how the
   hypotheses would be used to reach that conclusion. Name the key step.

4. **Locate the failure.** For the option you reject, point to the specific
   step that does not go through: a hypothesis that is not available, an
   estimate that loses a factor, a quantifier that cannot be exchanged, an
   endpoint the method cannot reach, a converse that needs a separate
   argument. If you cannot name such a step, do not reject the option merely
   because it is stronger.

5. **Check the other options quickly.** Each must either fail at a named step
   or be implied by your choice.

6. **Answer.** Prefer the strongest conclusion whose proof sketch survives.
   Keep the whole reply short, then give the answer line.
